## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-tier industrial corporate presence that fuses the structural solidity of heavy manufacturing with the refined discipline of contemporary luxury architecture. Designed to serve structural engineers, infrastructure developers, enterprise procurement officers, and institutional partners, the interface projects monumental strength, metallurgical expertise, and rigorous governance.

The aesthetic philosophy is **Architectural Industrial Minimalism**:
- **Monolithic Balance:** Interfaces adopt structural framing, disciplined rectangular planes, and intentional negative space evocative of modern steel and glass architecture.
- **Precision Engineering:** Layouts reject frivolous ornamentation in favor of crisp technical alignments, metallic micro-borders, and tactile interaction cues reminiscent of calibrated industrial instrumentation.
- **Controlled Warmth:** A foundational baseline of deep oceanic navy and structural charcoal is punctuated by a high-tensile copper-orange accent, evoking the molten heat of the foundry and refined copper detailing in architectural facade design.
- **Trust & Permanence:** Every view communicates institutional reliability, longevity, and industrial scale through meticulous typographic hierarchy and deliberate visual pacing.

## Colors

The design system defaults to a dark mode palette rooted in deep metallurgical depths, with a complementary high-contrast light mode suite tailored for specification sheets and enterprise data tables.

### Core Roles
- **Primary Surface / Deep Navy (`#101D2B`):** The foundational substrate for page framing, high-impact hero displays, mastheads, and primary visual anchors.
- **Secondary Surface / Structural Charcoal (`#1B2733`):** Applied to elevated cards, modular panels, navigation drawers, and secondary grouping containers.
- **Accent / Molten Copper (`#D97736`):** Reserved for primary interactive conversion points, operational highlights, status indicators, and subtle rim-lighting borders. Never overused; applied with surgical precision.
- **Neutral / Muted Slate Gray (`#87919B`):** The structural line work, metadata tags, secondary captions, and hairline perimeter dividers.
- **Light Field / Soft Background (`#F4F6F8`):** Utilized for high-density document segments, invoice tables, physical product specification viewports, and light-theme environments.
- **Pure White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-contrast headline text against dark surfaces, primary card fills within light contexts, and crisp specular highlights.

### Interaction States
- **Copper Hover (`#E68A4C`):** 10% luminous elevation for interactive copper elements.
- **Copper Active / Pressed (`#B85E24`):** Saturated deep tone for tactile depression feedback.
- **Subtle Surface Hover (`#243342`):** Discrete highlight layer for charcoal containers upon pointer focus.
- **Structural Border Tint (`rgba(135, 145, 155, 0.18)`): Low-contrast hairline dividers that maintain structural boundary clarity without visual friction.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes condensed, industrial architectural headlines with an immaculate, objective body system and technical data labels.

- **Headings (Barlow Condensed):** Evoking cold-rolled steel stencils, civil engineering schematics, and structural warehouse identifiers. Capitalized or heavy title-cased headers must use tight negative letter-spacing for large scales to generate architectural density.
- **Body & Editorial (Inter):** Guarantees frictionless reading for complex product dimensions, metallurgic grades (e.g., Fe 500D, Fe 550D), procurement terms, and institutional descriptions.
- **Technical & Labels (Space Grotesk):** Provides monospaced precision for technical load-bearing tables, grade identifiers, stock levels, and micro-navigation uppercase badges.
- **Rule of Case:** Section eyebrow headers and classification tags are strictly rendered in uppercase via `label-caps` with expanded tracking (`0.12em`) to balance the verticality of Barlow Condensed.

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout uses an architectural 12-column fluid grid governed by strict geometric rhythm and proportional alignment.

### Grid Foundations
- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12 columns, `margin-desktop` (4rem / 64px), `gutter-lg` (2.5rem / 40px). Max content width constrained to 1440px for editorial views and 1680px for procurement and data dashboard consoles.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8 columns, `margin` (2rem / 32px), `gutter` (1.5rem / 24px). Dual-column cards collapse to single-width or equal 4-column splits.
- **Mobile (≤ 767px):** 4 columns, `margin-mobile` (1.25rem / 20px), `gutter-sm` (1rem / 16px). All content stacks vertically; specification grids shift into horizontal swipe strips with visible snap points.

### Spacing Cadence
Spacing follows an intentional 8px base rhythm:
- `space-xs` (4px): Micro-spacing between icons and associated text labels.
- `space-sm` (8px): Internal padding for compact pills, list tags, and row item separation.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard component internal padding and field-label gutters.
- `space-lg` (24px): Inter-card gap and group offsets.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section divisions, major header-to-content buffers, and monolithic panel spacing.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids generic, fuzzy drop shadows. Depth is achieved via **structural tonal stacking, hairline metallic borders, and cinematic ambient occlusion**.

### Depth Layers
1. **Foundation Level (Elevation 0):** Deep Navy (`#101D2B`). The ground layer representing the primary structure.
2. **Structural Enclosure (Elevation 1):** Charcoal (`#1B2733`). Raised cards, navigation bars, and data blocks. Elevated purely by tone and bounded by a 1px border of Slate Gray at 18% opacity (`rgba(135, 145, 155, 0.18)`).
3. **Floating Console (Elevation 2):** Flyouts, dropdown menus, and popovers. Rendered in Charcoal (`#1B2733`) supported by a crisp 1px border and a directional shadow: `0 16px 32px -8px rgba(0, 5, 11, 0.60)`.
4. **Focused Modals (Elevation 3):** Lightened Charcoal (`#222F3D`) accompanied by a molten copper rim accent: `0 0 0 1px rgba(217, 119, 54, 0.4)`, layered over an ambient shadow: `0 24px 64px -12px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.85)`.

### Rim Lighting & Surface Sheen
Interactive surfaces feature an understated metallic gradient border or top-edge highlight (`1px linear-gradient(90deg, rgba(217,119,54,0.6) 0%, rgba(135,145,155,0.2) 100%)`). This replicates the reflection of light sweeping across precision-milled raw structural steel.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a **Soft Industrial Geometry** (`roundedness: 1`), keeping elements crisp, technical, and structural. Roundedness is kept to an absolute minimum to preserve architectural discipline and avoid casual, toy-like forms.

- **Base Components (0.25rem / 4px):** Standard buttons, text inputs, technical chips, badges, and table elements.
- **Modular Cards & Panels (0.5rem / 8px):** Structural container cards, interactive feature pods, and data modals (`rounded-lg`).
- **Heavy Containers & Hero Sections (0.75rem / 12px):** Reserved exclusively for major content sections (`rounded-xl`) when framed within full-bleed canvases.
- **Rule of Sharpness:** Interactive indicators, tabs, data points, and status icons utilize 0px (sharp) right angles to accentuate technical precision and engineering rigor. Pill or circular geometry is strictly prohibited except for numeric notification counts.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Industrial Action:** Copper Orange (`#D97736`) solid background, Pure White (`#FFFFFF`) bold label, 4px border radius. Hover introduces a subtle copper glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(217, 119, 54, 0.35)`). Active state applies a 1px downward translation.
- **Secondary Structural Action:** Deep Navy or Charcoal surface with a 1px solid Slate Gray border (`rgba(135, 145, 155, 0.3)`), Pure White label. Hover upgrades the border to solid Copper Orange (`#D97736`) with zero background change.
- **Ghost Action:** Pure White text, no background or border. Accompanied by a directional technical arrow indicator (`→`) that shifts 4px forward on hover.

### Cards
- **Structural Cards:** Charcoal (`#1B2733`) surface, 8px border radius, 1px perimeter border (`rgba(135, 145, 155, 0.18)`), internal padding of `space-lg` (24px).
- **Hover Reaction:** Cards subtly lift via a top-edge 1px highlight shifting to Copper Orange and an elevation increase of 2px with an ambient shadow (`0 12px 28px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`).
- **Metric Cards:** Charcoal container featuring an architectural top accent hairline (1px solid Copper Orange) with metric figures rendered in Barlow Condensed Display at high scale.

### Chips & Status Badges
- **Technical Spec Chips:** Dark background with 1px border (`rgba(135, 145, 155, 0.25)`), uppercase `label-caps` typography in Slate Gray (`#87919B`), 4px border radius.
- **Grade & Quality Badges (e.g., TMT 500D, IS 1786):** Subtle Copper tint background (`rgba(217, 119, 54, 0.12)`), Copper Orange border (`rgba(217, 119, 54, 0.4)`), Copper Orange text.

### Form Inputs
- **Base State:** Deep Navy (`#101D2B`) inset surface, 1px solid border (`rgba(135, 145, 155, 0.25)`), 4px border radius. Text rendered in Pure White, placeholder text in Slate Gray (`#87919B`).
- **Focus State:** 1px solid Copper Orange (`#D97736`) perimeter accompanied by a sharp 2px outer glow (`rgba(217, 119, 54, 0.2)`). No default browser outlines.
- **Labels:** Uppercase `label-caps` in Slate Gray positioned above the input field with `space-xs` margin.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Geometry:** Checkboxes use a 2px radius square; radio buttons use a crisp circle.
- **Resting:** Inset Charcoal surface with a 1.5px Slate Gray border.
- **Selected:** Solid Copper Orange (`#D97736`) fill with a Pure White centered checkmark or inner core dot.

### Data Tables & Spec Sheets
- **Header:** Uppercase `label-caps` on Deep Navy, divided from the body by a 1px solid Slate Gray separator.
- **Rows:** Alternating subtle Charcoal tints with a 1px bottom border. Hover row state triggers a low-opacity Copper highlight strip along the leading edge (3px solid `#D97736`).
- **Numeric Values:** Right-aligned and typeset in `Space Grotesk` or `Inter` with tabular figures enabled (`font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`).
