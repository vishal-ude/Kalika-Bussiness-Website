OUR MANUFACTURING CAPABILITIES

ENGINEERING QUALITY. DELIVERING RELIABILITY.

Kalika Industries operates advanced manufacturing facilities dedicated to high-specification corrugated fiber-cement building components. By utilizing continuous slurry homogenization and controlled steam curing, each panel provides uncompromised structural integrity for modern industrial installations.

01 blender

Raw Material Prep & Slurry Homogenization

Selected mineral fibers, Portland cement, and silica aggregates are measured via digital load cells and uniformly dispersed in high-velocity mixers.

Stage: Automated Wet End
02 reorder

Sheet Forming & Corrugation Mold

De-watered laminates are drawn over continuous vacuum mandrels and guided through calibrated corrugation templates for uniform wave pitch.

Stage: Roll Geometry Mold
03 humidity_mid

Controlled Curing & Stabilization

Green sheets are stacked into micro-climate autoclaves and saturated curing yards for hydration, eliminating internal shrinkage cracks.

Stage: Hydration Chambers
04 verified_user

Dimension Inspection & Palletizing

Every production cycle undergoes flexural bending break analysis, 24-hr water impermeability tests, edge trim rectification, and barcode batching.

Stage: QA Verification & Dispatch
